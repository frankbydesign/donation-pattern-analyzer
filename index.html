Concentration Risk Analysis

Revenue Concentration by Top Donors

Shows what percentage of total revenue comes from your top N donors. A steep curve indicates high concentration risk—if the line reaches 50%+ with just 10 donors, your revenue is dangerously dependent on a few individuals. Use this to prioritize donor diversification and plan for major donor attrition scenarios.

Giving Level Distribution

Health nonprofits typically see 60-70% of donors in the under-$100 tier. A higher share of mid-level ($100-$500) donors suggests strong cultivation.

Donor Health & Segmentation

Donor Segments (RFM-Based)

Segments donors by Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value. Champions and Loyal Customers (green/blue) are your core—aim for 30%+ here. Red/orange segments (At Risk, Lost) need immediate attention. Use this to tailor outreach strategies and allocate stewardship resources by segment.

Lapse Risk Distribution

Lapse risk predicts which donors may stop giving based on their historical patterns. High-risk donors gave consistently before but have gone quiet. These are your best reactivation candidates—they already believe in the mission.

At-Risk High-Value Donors

These donors have contributed significantly but show signs of lapsing. Priority for retention outreach.

Temporal Trends & Retention

Cohort Retention Analysis

Each line tracks donors acquired in a specific year through their giving journey. Read left-to-right: Year 1 shows first-year retention (industry benchmark: 20-25%), with gradual improvement expected in later years. Lines trending upward indicate improving loyalty; flat or declining lines signal retention problems. Compare cohorts to identify which acquisition strategies produced the most loyal donors.

Upgrade/Downgrade Trends

This shows how many donors increased their giving (upgrades), decreased (downgrades), or stayed the same compared to their previous year. A healthy nonprofit sees more green than red. Large downgrade spikes may indicate donor fatigue or external economic pressure.

What Each Severity Level Means
RED - Critical
  • Exact duplicates (same donor, date, amount)
  • Impossible values ($0 or negative)
  • Future-dated donations
  • Extreme outliers (20x+ above average)
ORANGE - Warning
  • Near-duplicates (within 2 days)
  • Large outliers (10-20x above average)
  • Data entry lag over 30 days
YELLOW - Info
  • Moderate outliers (3-10x above average)
  • Pattern breaks (inconsistent giving)
  • Stale single-gift donors (2+ years inactive)
